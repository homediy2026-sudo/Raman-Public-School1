About Our School

Building Futures Since Our Founding

Raman Public School is a general education English medium institution located in the heart of Sahjanwa, Uttar Pradesh. Established with a vision to provide quality education to children of the region, our school has grown into a trusted institution for families across Ghanshyam Nagar and surrounding areas.

Our aim is to provide a strong academic foundation while instilling discipline, responsibility, and confidence in every student. We focus on nurturing students intellectually, socially, and morally to prepare them for future challenges.

Our Academic Program

Our academic program is designed to build strong fundamentals and promote conceptual clarity. We follow a student-friendly approach that makes learning engaging and effective.

📝

Structured Syllabus

Carefully designed NCERT-aligned curriculum that builds knowledge progressively through each grade.

📊

Regular Assessments

🎯

Student-Friendly Teaching

Interactive teaching methods that encourage participation, questions, and creative problem-solving.

Our Facilities

A safe, clean, and well-equipped campus designed for quality learning

Campus & Infrastructure

Everything Your Child Needs to Thrive

We provide a comprehensive set of facilities that support both academic learning and the physical, creative, and social development of our students.

🏫

Spacious Classrooms

Well-ventilated, well-lit classrooms equipped with blackboards, furniture, and learning materials. Each room is designed to accommodate optimal class sizes for effective learning and personal teacher attention.

📚

School Library

A well-stocked library containing academic textbooks, reference books, story books, and educational materials across all subjects and grade levels to encourage reading habits and independent research.

⚽

Playground & Sports

A dedicated playground area for physical activities, sports, and outdoor games. Regular PE classes and sports activities help students build fitness, teamwork, and a healthy lifestyle.

🔒

Safe & Secure Campus

Our campus is enclosed with a secure boundary wall and gated entrance, ensuring students are safe throughout the school day. Disciplined environment with responsible supervision at all times.

🚰

Clean Environment

Maintained clean classrooms, corridors, and common areas with proper sanitation facilities for both boys and girls. Hygiene and cleanliness are given high priority on our campus.

🌿

Student-Friendly Campus

A welcoming and nurturing environment where students feel comfortable to learn, play, and express themselves. A campus that truly feels like a second home for every child.
